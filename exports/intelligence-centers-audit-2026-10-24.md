## Original audit

Reviewed October 24, 2026 against the repository's published pages, the September 15 and October 6 supplied minutes, the October 20 transcript-based record, recent findings, and the completion configuration. Scope: 63 center and related root pages, nine named project/service/policy centers, and the seven service-area directory categories. This is a content and navigation audit, not a fresh search for later City or agency records.

## Main finding

Recent Council findings have been appended without consistently updating each center's main status, timeline, funding explanation, evidence register, and questions. Readers can therefore see an outdated overview even when a recent action is recorded farther down the page.

The October 20 police-station action is already present in `police.html` and `police-facilities.html`, in the lower Additional Council evidence section. The prominent overview still sends readers to August 2025/May 2026 planning discussions. Police History, Evidence, and Questions do not yet integrate the October 20 action.

## Technical and editorial defects

1. `animal-control-sheltering.html` ends mid-sentence at line 494; the remaining body/footer is absent.
2. `animal-control-municipal-code.html` ends mid-sentence at line 186.
3. `potrero-interchange-history.html` ends mid-sentence at line 402.
4. Twelve center/related pages link minutes to `/viewer.html`, but the existing viewer is `/documents/viewer.html`. The old `build_recent_center_findings.py` PDF link path is one source of this problem. Repair the generator as well as its outputs.
5. Completion scores lack a last-reviewed date, a published rubric, and a separate currentness/technical-health measure. Research completeness should not be inflated by historical breadth while current status and essential source links remain stale.
6. Top-level status cards, appended findings, evidence catalogs and questions are edited through different paths. A dated source record should drive all affected destinations or a checklist should require each destination to be updated.
