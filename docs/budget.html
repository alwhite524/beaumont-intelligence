Read with care

Financial terms are not interchangeable

Budget or appropriation

Authority to spend up to an approved amount. It is not proof that the money was spent.

Contract authorization

Permission to enter into an agreement. Payments may occur later and may be less than the authorized ceiling.

Grant award

A funding commitment that may depend on agreements, deadlines, eligible costs, reimbursement, or matching funds.

Actual expenditure

A recorded payment or recognized expense. Audited actuals provide stronger year-end confirmation than interim reports.
