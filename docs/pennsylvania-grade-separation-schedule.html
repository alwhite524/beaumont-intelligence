First major delay

Environmental clearance moved nearly 11 months

Environmental approval became one of the most consequential schedule issues in the project.

Original approach

The project initially advanced using a CEQA statutory-exemption approach.

Additional environmental document

Caltrans Environmental later required Beaumont to prepare an Initial Study / Mitigated Negative Declaration.

Additional work completed

The City and its consultants performed additional environmental work in response to that direction.

Approach changes again

Following further coordination, Caltrans and Beaumont ultimately returned to the earlier statutory-exemption approach.

California Transportation Commission records later attributed nearly 11 months of delay to this environmental-review sequence.
November 2025

Environmental approval arrives after the original construction date

Beaumont approved the Pennsylvania Avenue environmental record and project on November 18, 2025.

That is important because the original 2024 schedule had projected that construction would already be starting in November 2025.

Second major transition

Project delivery moves to RCTC

Environmental delay was not the only schedule issue.

By late 2025, the project still required substantial final design, right-of-way, utility, railroad, and agency-coordination work.

Beaumont requests assistance

Beaumont asked RCTC to help deliver Pennsylvania because of critical timing requirements associated with the state construction funding.

RCTC assumes lead delivery

RCTC began coordinating final design and right-of-way delivery.

Consultant team consolidated

HDR was selected to lead the consolidated PS&E effort under RCTC.

Transition takes time

State records attribute approximately one additional month of delay to onboarding the new RCTC-led consultant team.

Ready-to-List

What does June 2027 actually mean?

Construction-ready procurement milestone

Ready-to-List generally means that the plans, specifications, estimates, approvals, and related project-delivery requirements have advanced sufficiently for the project to proceed toward construction advertisement.

It is an important milestone, but it is not the same as:

  • groundbreaking,
  • construction contract award,
  • notice to proceed,
  • physical excavation beginning,
  • or project completion.
Beaumont Intelligence standard: Until a construction contract is awarded and construction is formally authorized, this center will describe Pennsylvania Avenue as being in project development rather than “under construction.”
Preparing early

Construction management can begin before construction

RCTC has identified Pennsylvania Avenue construction management as a planned procurement.

That does not mean the construction contract itself has been awarded.

Why procure early?

A construction-management team can assist with constructability review, bid preparation, schedule planning, railroad coordination, utility sequencing, and preconstruction activities.

What happens later?

Once construction is awarded, the construction-management team typically supports administration, inspection, documentation, schedule review, and contractor coordination.

Construction complexity

Building the project will require careful sequencing

Even after a construction contract is awarded, Pennsylvania Avenue cannot simply be excavated all at once.

1

Temporary railroad operations

Rail traffic must be maintained while the permanent railroad bridge and grade separation are constructed.

2

Utility relocation

Conflicting utilities may need to be relocated before major excavation and structural work.

3

Roadway excavation

Pennsylvania Avenue must be lowered while access and surrounding transportation systems are managed.

4

Railroad bridge

The permanent structure must be constructed and approved for railroad operations.

5

I-10 ramp coordination

Work near the freeway must remain coordinated with Caltrans and existing interstate traffic.

6

Final traffic configuration

Roadway, signals, pedestrian facilities, utilities, and the permanent rail alignment must ultimately be placed into their final configuration.

Intelligence assessment

Pennsylvania Avenue's schedule is a project-delivery story

The original Pennsylvania Avenue schedule did not fail because of one isolated event.

Environmental processing alone added nearly a year to the schedule.

At the same time, final design remained incomplete, right-of-way and utility work had not been fully advanced, railroad coordination remained a major long-lead activity, and the project was approaching a state construction-funding deadline.

That combination led Beaumont to bring RCTC into a lead delivery role and led RCTC to seek additional time from the California Transportation Commission.

The present objective is therefore not simply “start construction.”

The immediate objective is to complete the work necessary to place a technically complex, fully coordinated, adequately funded project into construction procurement without jeopardizing the $49.4 million state award.
