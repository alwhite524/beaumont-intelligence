Wastewater Treatment Plant Improvements

The civil and landscape construction packages for improvements at Beaumont’s wastewater treatment plant, CIP WW24-01.

Key records and decisions

March 18, 2025 · Rebidding

Council rejected all bids for the first package and authorized staff to revise the bid documents and re-advertise.

Council record and original procurement materials →

September 2, 2025 · Construction awards

Council unanimously awarded the civil package to Innovative Construction Solutions for $3,383,857 and the landscape package to Southern California Landscaping for $598,905. Including authorized contingencies, the combined ceiling for the two packages was $4,381,038.

Council minutes, item J.9 →

October 2025 · Mobilization

The City Manager’s monthly report noted that the contractor was mobilizing, submitting materials for review, and ordering equipment.

October City Manager report →

Approved change orders, actual payments, and final acceptance for each construction package.
