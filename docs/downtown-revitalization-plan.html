Plan & Design

The physical concepts, mobility questions, and studies required before construction decisions.

Current statusLong-range plan adopted; preliminary engineering directed; final scope, cost, and construction remain separate decisions.
OverviewHistoryPlan & DesignBusiness ProgramsFundingCouncil ActionsEvidence

Gateways and identity

Grace Avenue gateway concepts, downtown arches, wayfinding, public art, coordinated signage, landscaping, and lighting.

Sixth Street and Beaumont Avenue

Pedestrian-focused streetscapes, parallel or median parking concepts, bicycle facilities, crossings, landscaping, and potential lane changes.

Dining, civic, and mixed-use hubs

The Arbor dining concept, civic gathering space, housing above shops, public events, and a stronger day-and-evening downtown population.

Roundabouts and mobility

Conceptual roundabouts at California Avenue and Palm Avenue were presented as traffic-calming features, subject to technical analysis and design.

Key unresolved issue: Council discussion repeatedly focused on through traffic, freeway detours, truck routes, Fifth Street and other alternatives, parking, and whether capacity changes could shift congestion. The authorized studies were intended to quantify conditions and inform design; they do not predetermine a construction outcome.
