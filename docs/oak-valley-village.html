Oak Valley Village

The Regency Centers retail development at Oak Valley Parkway and I-10, its development agreement, and the off-site traffic reimbursement decision.

Key records and decisions

June 17, 2025 · First reading and reimbursement

Council voted 3–0 to advance the development agreement with Beaumont Regency AVG LLC, approve an off-site traffic reimbursement agreement contingent on the development agreement, and amend the capital plan to provide a maximum of $6 million from Streets & Bridges Development Impact Fees. The ceiling applies to eligible traffic improvements under the agreement.

First reading, reimbursement agreement, and capital plan action →

July 15, 2025 · Second reading

Item G.13 returned for second reading. The minutes record Council Member Fenn’s abstention but omit G.13 from the printed consent approval range. The reviewed captions indicate broader consent approval and acknowledge the abstention. The signed ordinance would resolve the ambiguity in the published record.

Second-reading packet, minutes, and caption comparison →

September 2025 · Development milestones

The City identified Regency Centers as the developer of the approximately 28-acre retail site and announced Target as its anchor. The September City Manager report recorded receipt of a plan for a separate shell building west of the planned Target.
