Evidence standard

From summary to source

Beaumont Intelligence is designed so residents can move from a plain-English explanation to the underlying public record.

As the platform develops, each Intelligence Center will maintain a dedicated source register containing direct links to official City records, Council documents, contracts, budgets, outside government agencies, and other supporting evidence.
