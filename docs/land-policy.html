How Beaumont established PLUS

PLUS means Policy on Land Use and Sensitive Receptors. This center follows the public record behind Beaumont's response to warehouse and logistics development: early mitigation discussions, the September 2022 adoption, clarification of applicability, community-benefit amendments and the March 2025 AB 98 revision.

Key decisions

2022-09-06 · 13

Council establishes and names PLUS

Adopted · unanimous motions

Council unanimously accepted redlines to the draft land-use policy for logistics, warehouse, distribution and similar industrial development with a 1,000-foot setback stipulation. Fenn moved and Martinez seconded. A separate unanimous motion by Lara, seconded by White, named the policy PLUS.

Evidence limit: The October clarification identifies September 6 as the adoption date. These minutes establish the motions; the original adopted policy is still needed for a complete version comparison.

Official minutes

2024-02-06 · J.3

Community-benefit and project requirements amended

Amendments adopted · unanimous

Council unanimously adopted the updated PLUS as presented on White's motion, seconded by Fenn. The item described formalizing the Community Benefit Fee and updating Siting and Design, Operations and Construction Phase requirements.

Evidence limit: Adoption does not establish the amount collected, the balance available or how community-benefit funds were spent.

Official minutes

2025-03-04 · G.9

AB 98 revisions adopted

Amendments adopted · unanimous

Council unanimously adopted PLAN2024-0134, the amended PLUS presented for compliance with Assembly Bill 98, on Lara's motion, seconded by White. The policy linked from the City's Planning page carries this date.

Evidence limit: This is the latest policy version located for this center. It does not by itself establish subsequent enforcement, project compliance or the current status of state law.
