Research status

A long-range downtown vision is moving into implementation decisions.

Beaumont's adopted Elevate 2050 concept describes a more walkable, mixed-use downtown supported by streetscape improvements, public spaces, business activity, housing, mobility changes, and a stronger sense of place.

The concept is not a single construction project or guaranteed schedule. Individual studies, designs, contracts, funding decisions, property actions, and construction phases require separate public records and Council actions.

What the vision includes

More than streetscape improvements

The adopted concept connects physical design with economic activity and community use.

Walkable streets and public space

Sidewalks, crossings, landscaping, lighting, public art, wayfinding, gathering areas, and potential mobility changes.

Business and investment

Business attraction and expansion programs, dining and retail activity, façade improvements, and strategic public investment.

Housing and mixed use

Long-range opportunities for housing, services, shops, and daily activity within the downtown area.

Initial Council milestones

1

Elevate 2050 plan adopted

On September 17, 2024, Council adopted the Downtown Beaumont Revitalization Plan as presented, 3–0. The official minutes and complete plan are linked in the history.

Review the adoption record →
2

Preliminary engineering direction

On August 19, 2025, Council voted 3–2 to direct staff to move forward with all six preliminary-engineering tasks, including investigations, traffic analysis, design work, and a preliminary design report.

3

Economic strategy adopted

On August 4, 2026, Council adopted the 2026–2031 Economic Development Strategic Plan 5–0. Its initiatives include business retention, targeted industries, strategic sites, quality of place, and City promotion.

4

Downtown business incentive

At the same August 2026 meeting, Council approved a $50,000 forgivable loan for Tuscano's Pizza & Pasta through the Downtown Business Attraction and Expansion Program.

Questions this center will track

From vision to accountable implementation

What is approved?

Distinguish the adopted concept from later studies, designs, contracts, property actions, and construction awards.

What will it cost?

Track planning, property, design, incentive, infrastructure, and construction amounts without combining unlike figures.

How will access change?

Follow traffic analysis, alternate routes, parking, pedestrian safety, truck routes, and the relationship to other transportation projects.

Evidence standard: This center links the adopted plan, official minutes, meeting records, staff reports, and local transcripts. Missing contract outcomes, current accounting, and implementation records remain labeled without presenting proposals as completed work.
