How the dated score works

Each completed check earns 20 points; incomplete or unverified checks earn zero. Score = completed checks ÷ 5 × 100. A source route earns discovery credit without certifying every attachment. Minutes credit requires a minutes-based review. The final-instrument check requires both reconciliation of material discrepancies and review of the applicable final agreements, ordinances or resolutions. Each score reflects the evidence available on its review date.

  1. Dated action and service-area scope recorded
  2. Decision record and supporting-document route linked
  3. Disposition checked against reviewed City minutes
  4. Financial authority distinguished from estimates and expenditure
  5. Final instruments and material discrepancies reconciled

A 100% checklist would complete this bounded action review only. Payments, delivery and service outcomes require their own evidence. Cross-listed actions retain one score and count once in the register total.
