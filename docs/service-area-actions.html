Follow selected Council decisions by civic service, with dated research checks and the next records needed.

This initial register covers nine selected August–October 2026 actions. It is not a complete inventory of Council decisions or service performance. Scores describe research readiness for each action, not construction progress, factual certainty or permission to publish.

How the dated score works

Each completed check earns 20 points; incomplete or unverified checks earn zero. Score = completed checks ÷ 5 × 100. A source route earns discovery credit without certifying every attachment. Minutes credit requires a minutes-based review. The final-instrument check remains open until corrected agreements, resolutions and material financial discrepancies are reconciled. Every score is an October 24 snapshot and must be reviewed when new evidence arrives.

  1. Dated action and service-area scope recorded
  2. Decision record and supporting-document route linked
  3. Disposition checked against reviewed City minutes
  4. Financial authority distinguished from estimates and expenditure
  5. Final instruments and material discrepancies reconciled

A 100% checklist would complete this bounded action review only. Payments, delivery and service outcomes require their own evidence. Cross-listed actions retain one score and count once in the nine-action total; area totals overlap.

Short-term rental ordinance introduced; fees approved

Research completion: 60% · 3/5 checks

Council voted to introduce the short-term rental ordinance and adopt the fee resolution. Staff presented a revised ordinance with $500/$1,000/$2,000 fine tiers and a 15-day correction interval; the occupancy discussion clarified that three bedrooms allow six occupants. Staff targeted January 2027 implementation and described a later second reading.

Evidence: Transcript-based; approved October 20 minutes not collected

Limit: Introduction is not final ordinance adoption. Revised ordinance text and the executed fee resolution were not collected; archived packet discrepancies remain documented below. The captioned nine-person example was corrected to six during discussion.
