Issue history

Billboards in Beaumont

Two related but different stories run through the record: the City’s use of billboards for public and economic-development advertising, and City policy governing static signs, digital conversions, and relocation agreements.

Current evidence boundary: This history covers the 36 Council transcripts and official records presently indexed by Beaumont Intelligence. The September 1 meeting transcript is not yet in the archive, so its final discussion, motion, and vote remain to be verified.
Policy and property decisions

Digital conversion and relocation

City Manager reportFeb. 3, 2026

Community survey update

Staff reported nearly 200 responses. About 70% were described as neutral or supportive of billboards for community messaging; concerns included neighborhood proximity, brightness, driver distraction, and visual clutter. Council clarified that the survey concerned electronic billboards.
