Beaumont Sustainability Plan

The adopted Sustainability Plan, its emissions baseline, and the measures the City can track through later budgets and implementation reports.

Key records and decisions

September 1, 2026 · Plan adopted

Council voted 5–0 to adopt the Sustainability Plan and the related General Plan environmental addendum resolution. The review identifies 37 sustainability measures and 21 resilience measures over a five- to ten-year horizon.

Council decision and hearing record → · City plan and fact sheets →

2022 · Community emissions baseline

The 2022 inventory attributed 63% of community greenhouse gas emissions to transportation and 28% to building energy. These figures establish the baseline for comparing later inventories.
