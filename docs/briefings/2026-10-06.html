October 6, 2026 City Council meeting

Traffic-signal upgrades, playgrounds and field lighting, temporary skate amenities, and a proposed 2027 bicycle event.

Upcoming · No meeting outcomes yet

Tuesday, October 6, 2026
Closed session: 5:00 PM · Regular meeting: 6:00 PM
550 E. Sixth Street, Beaumont, CA

Open interactive agendaOfficial City agendaView full agenda packet

Prepared October 3 from the City’s published agenda and supplied 1,803-page packet. All amounts and actions below are proposals, not approvals. The interactive agenda links all 70 City-listed supporting documents across 20 voting items.

Participate in the meeting

Attend in person or use the City livestream. The agenda accepts comments by email to nicolew@beaumontca.gov with “Public Comment” in the subject, by phone at (951) 922-4845, or in person using a Request to Speak form. Comments generally have a three-minute limit; see the official agenda for submission timing and accessibility assistance.

Closed session, presentations, and reports

Closed session includes four existing-litigation matters and one potential case (B.1–B.5). E.1 introduces and swears in Officer Kristin Pisciuneri. The agenda also includes public comment, Youth Council, legislative updates, City Manager and Council reports, and future agenda items. See the official agenda for the complete sequence and closed-session case descriptions.

J.5 · Temporary skate amenities at Trevino Park

Consider up to $120,000 to competitively bid and install temporary bolt-down skate features on one of Trevino Park’s two basketball courts while the Stewart Park skatepark is closed for construction. The second court would remain available for basketball. Staff identifies CIP funding without an additional General Fund appropriation.

Official documents (2)
  • Staff Report - Trevino Park Skate Amenities
  • Attachment A - American Ramp Proposal
