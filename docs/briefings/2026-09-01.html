Executive overview

One continuation, one tabled item, and six unanimous approvals

Council adopted the Sustainability Plan and e-conveyance rules, continued the short-term-rental ordinance for revisions, approved the Chamber agreement and sponsorship policy, tabled the Lamar billboard agreement for more negotiation, and advanced a 15-date 2027 Beaumont Nights program with a licensed beer garden.

I.1 · Public hearing · Sustainability

2026 Beaumont Sustainability Plan

Final Council actionAdopted the Sustainability Plan and the related General Plan EIR addendum resolution.
ActionAdopted
Vote5–0
Video1:31:53–1:52:52 ↗

The five-to-ten-year strategy includes 37 community sustainability measures and 21 resilience measures. Council discussed the state requirement, emissions accounting, Interstate 10 pass-through traffic, and the approximately $300,000 plan budget.

Record note

Transportation accounted for 63% of the plan's 2022 community greenhouse-gas inventory and building energy accounted for 28%. Later implementation actions will require separate feasibility, budget, and policy review.

I.2 · Public hearing · Neighborhood regulation

Short-Term Rental Program and Ordinance

Final Council actionContinued the first reading to the first October meeting so staff could revise the proposed language.
ActionContinued
Vote5–0
Video1:52:52–2:33:28 ↗

The proposal would create annual licensing, tax registration, occupancy and parking rules, quiet hours, local-contact requirements, inspections, and enforcement. Council requested clearer enforcement timing and escalating-fine language before taking final action.

Not adopted September 1

The proposed $250 annual license and related fee and penalty schedules remained proposals because the item was continued.

I.3 · Public hearing · Public safety

E-Bikes and E-Conveyances Ordinance

Final Council actionWaived the full reading, introduced the ordinance, and adopted the recommended administrative-citation schedule.
ActionApproved
Vote5–0
Video2:33:28–2:48:43 ↗

The ordinance establishes operating and equipment rules for bicycles, e-bikes, scooters, motorized skateboards, and similar devices, including a 5 mph sidewalk limit and restrictions on electric off-highway motorcycles in public areas.

Public-safety focus

Council emphasized education and compliance while recognizing the serious injuries that can result from high-speed e-conveyance collisions.
