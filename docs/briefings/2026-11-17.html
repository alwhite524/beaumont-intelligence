J.3 · Stewart Park Phase II construction bid package

Approve Phase II plans and specifications and authorize advertisement for construction bids. Proposed amenities include a secondary playground, skate park work, basketball/pickleball courts, picnic shelters, a stage bandshell, paths, lighting and landscaping. This is bid authorization, not a construction contract award.

Funding and fiscal context: Staff reports no fiscal impact from this recommended action and anticipates a 45-day advertisement period with bids in January 2027. The separate Phase I schedule identifies March 2027 completion with potential weather delays.

Keep Phase I completion separate from Phase II bidding

The March 2027 completion statement is in the Phase I progress update. Phase II is proposed for bid solicitation, with bids anticipated in January 2027. Do not present March 2027 as a confirmed completion date for the full park or Phase II. Attachment F is a one-page contract-drawings reference; the Phase II bid plan set is Attachment I, pages 1249–1313.

G.10 · Axon cameras, licenses and bundled AI services

Consider a $130,314.94 Axon addition for eight body-worn cameras and 12 Pro user licenses. The attached quote also includes AI Assistant, Draft One report writing, transcription, redaction and Fusus livestream licenses.

Funding and fiscal context: Staff identifies the Police operating budget and future budget cycles. The quote spans January 2027–February 2031.

Quote scope includes AI tools and a different payment date

The staff report describes a four-year camera/license addition. The quote specifies 50 months, includes $40,512 for AI Assistant and Draft One, and lists transcription, redaction and Fusus licenses. Its June 1, 2028 payment date differs from July 2028 in the staff report; the total agrees. Clarify the authorized service scope, policy safeguards and controlling payment schedule.

G.16 · First-quarter budget results and adjustment

Receive and file first-quarter budget results and approve the accompanying budget adjustment. Staff reports General Fund spending of $15.2 million, about 19.3% of the $78.5 million budget; the recommended adjustment table totals $1,643,493.

Adjustment descriptions need reconciliation

The report's summary labels a $53,000 Planning increase as Weed Abatement, while the adjustment form describes Golden Triangle annexation professional services. The summary totals $1,643,493; staff says the changes stay within existing resources. Ask Finance to reconcile the descriptions and distinguish appropriation changes, transfers and coding corrections before treating the total as new spending.
