Executive summary

Council adopted the new fiscal-year spending plan

The central action was unanimous adoption of the FY 2026–27 appropriation limit, operating budget, and five-year Capital Improvement Plan. Council also approved annual CFD tax levies, two fire-protection agreements, an interim deputy city manager contract, and employee memorandums of understanding.

Public Hearing I.1 · Budget / Capital Plan

FY 2026–27 operating budget and five-year CIP

Council actionAdopted the annual appropriation limit, operating budget, FY 2027–31 Capital Improvement Plan, related prior-year project list, and requisition list.
ActionAdopted
Vote5–0
Video1:33:05–1:44:18 ↗

Staff presented General Fund revenues and expenditures of $75.6 million each. Across all funds, projected revenues were $168 million and expenditures were $162.5 million. Staff explained that restricted funds, reserves, transfers, and equipment-replacement funding account for part of the all-fund difference.

BI Insight

These figures are adopted plans and legal spending authority. They are not proof of actual revenue, expenditure, or year-end surplus; those require later interim reports and audited statements.

Action Item J.1 · Community Facilities Districts

Annual CFD special-tax levies

Council actionAdopted five resolutions authorizing FY 2026–27 special-tax levies, with separate votes used where Council members recused themselves from particular improvement areas.
ActionAdopted
VoteUnanimous among participating members
Video1:45:29–2:00:59 ↗

Staff described approximately 34,920 charges on roughly 16,149 parcels, totaling about $30.8 million—approximately 2.25% more than the prior year. The proposed levy was described as about $679,000 below the maximum authorized amount.
