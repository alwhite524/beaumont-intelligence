J.2 · Transportation · Funding strategy

Pennsylvania Avenue Grade Separation

Decision required
Decision before CouncilAuthorize the City Manager to withdraw the project from the 2024 TCEP cycle and reapply in the 2026 cycle.

In one minute

Council will decide whether to withdraw the Pennsylvania Avenue Grade Separation from the 2024 TCEP cycle and reapply in 2026. Staff says this reduces the risk of losing the current award for missing the June 2027 allocation deadline while design, right-of-way, permitting, utilities and agency agreements continue.

Key points
  • The decision changes the funding strategy; it does not stop the project.
  • Design is approximately 65% complete.
  • The total project estimate is approximately $147.6 million.
  • Federal grant applications and other funding opportunities remain pending.
Fiscal impact

No construction appropriation is requested tonight. The packet reports an estimated total project budget of about $147.6 million and an unfunded amount of about $54.7 million.

What to watch
  • Why staff believes withdrawal is less risky than keeping the current award.
  • What amount and schedule are anticipated for the 2026 TCEP application.
  • How drainage, Union Pacific, Caltrans and utility dependencies affect the revised schedule.

J.3 · Economic development · Strategic plan

2026–2031 Economic Development Strategic Plan

Decision required
Decision before CouncilAdopt the 2026–2031 Economic Development Strategic Plan as presented.

In one minute

Council will consider adopting Beaumont’s 2026–2031 Economic Development Strategic Plan. The plan sets five-year direction for business retention, targeted industries, retail corridors, strategic land and sites, quality of place, and City promotion.

Key points
  • Updates the City’s 2019 Economic Development Strategic Plan.
  • Uses six strategic initiatives and an implementation matrix.
  • Adoption sets policy direction but does not approve every future program or expenditure.
  • Future actions requiring funding or contracts must return to Council.
Fiscal impact

Adoption has no additional immediate fiscal impact. The completed planning contract was not to exceed $110,000; future implementation costs will return separately.

What to watch
  • Which initiatives Council wants staff to prioritize first.
  • How progress will be measured and reported.
  • Which recommendations are expected to return as near-term agenda items.

G.13 · Finance · Fiscal-year closeout

FY 2025–26 Fourth-Quarter Budget and Financial Status

Receive and file
Action before CouncilReceive and file the report on the City’s budget and financial status as of June 30, 2026.

In one minute

Council will receive the fourth-quarter financial report for Fiscal Year 2025–26. The report provides the year-end view of revenues, expenditures, cash and investments before later year-end adjustments and audit work are completed.

Key points
  • Reviews year-end operating performance.
  • Includes cash and investment reporting.
  • Provides the starting point for the new fiscal year.
  • Does not itself authorize new spending.
Fiscal impact

This is a receive-and-file reporting item. Any budget amendments or new appropriations must return to Council separately.

What to watch
  • Whether major revenues finished above or below budget.
  • Whether reserve balances or fund projections changed.
  • Whether staff identifies year-end adjustments that will return to Council.

G.10 · Governance · Strategic priorities

2025–2027 Strategic Plan Priority Order

Decision required
Decision before CouncilApprove the revised priority order used for staff work plans, budget development and progress reporting.

In one minute

Council will consider a revised order for the City’s 2025–2027 Strategic Plan priorities. The update changes the order of major projects and consolidates Police Station design, funding and construction into one priority.

Key points
  • Pennsylvania remains the top priority.
  • The Police Department Station moves to second.
  • Stewart Park Phase 2 moves to third.
  • Approval does not appropriate money or award contracts.
Fiscal impact

No direct fiscal impact is created by changing the priority order. Project funding and contracts must return through separate agenda items.

What to watch
  • Whether Council changes the recommended order.
  • Whether any project is added, removed or consolidated.
  • Which priorities staff expects to bring back next.

J.1 · Downtown investment · Business incentive

Tuscano’s Pizza & Pasta Expansion

Decision required
Decision before CouncilApprove a $50,000 forgivable loan and find that it serves a valid public purpose.

In one minute

Council will consider a $50,000 forgivable loan for Tuscano’s Pizza & Pasta through the Downtown Business Attraction and Expansion Program. The proposal supports an approximately 1,000-square-foot covered patio and continued downtown investment.

Key points
  • Proposed assistance is $50,000.
  • Full forgiveness requires five years of compliant operation.
  • Partial forgiveness may occur after three years.
  • The agreement includes repayment provisions if requirements are not met.
Fiscal impact

The proposed $50,000 loan would be paid from the Downtown Business Attraction and Expansion Program account.

What to watch
  • What expenses are eligible under the agreement.
  • How performance and forgiveness requirements will be documented.
  • How Council evaluates the public benefit and risk.

I.2 · Public hearing · Continuance

2026 Sustainability Plan

Procedural continuance
Decision before CouncilContinue the public hearing to September 1, 2026.

In one minute

Council is being asked to continue the Sustainability Plan public hearing to September 1, 2026. Staff requests additional time to refine related ordinance amendments, findings and interdepartmental review.

Key points
  • No plan adoption is requested on August 4.
  • The proposed return date is September 1, 2026.
  • Additional ordinance and sign-regulation work remains underway.
  • The hearing remains open for future Council consideration.
Fiscal impact

Continuing the hearing has no additional fiscal impact.

What to watch
  • Whether Council confirms the September 1 return date.
  • Whether Council requests additional revisions.
  • Whether any public testimony changes the scope of the follow-up.
