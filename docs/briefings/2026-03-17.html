Executive summary

What Council decided

Council approved the consent calendar, a manhole-replacement contract, two $5,000 community sponsorships, and the 2026 Legislative Platform. It received the truck-prohibition update and directed staff to press for resolution of the Stewart Park grant delay before returning on May 5.
