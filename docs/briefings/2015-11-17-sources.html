A clarificationwas madethat petition signatures must be submitted to the Registrar by April 2016. Mr. Pinkney explained that if the petition qualifies per the Registrar, the Council may adopt the Ordinance that is beingpresentedintheinitiativeor submittheOrdinancetothevoters.
