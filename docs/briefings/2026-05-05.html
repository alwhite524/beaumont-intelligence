Executive summary

What Council decided

Council unanimously approved the entire consent calendar, the TEFRA financing resolution, major design and event-service agreements, and several infrastructure actions. Council received a Stewart Park progress report, directed staff toward an outsourced short-term-rental program, and removed the sponsorship-policy item for a later meeting.

Flyland Holdings TEFRA financing
