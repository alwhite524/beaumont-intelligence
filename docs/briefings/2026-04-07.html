Executive summary

What Council decided

Council unanimously approved the consent calendar and most action items, including a downtown business grant, investment-management contract, City Manager appointment, public-meeting disruption policy, a three-year Drone First Responder agreement, sewer-project closeout, Strategic Plan updates, and a wireless sublease. Items J.3 and J.4 were postponed to April 21.
