Upcoming · Council action pending

Closed session: 5:00 PM · Regular meeting: 6:00 PM

Attend and comment

Attend in person or watch the City livestream. Email nicolew@beaumontca.gov with “Public Comment” in the subject before or during the meeting, up to the end of the corresponding item; call (951) 922-4845 before the item; or submit an in-person Request to Speak form. Comments generally have a three-minute limit. For accessibility assistance, contact the Clerk at (951) 572-3196; the notice requests 48 hours’ advance notification.

Closed session, presentation, and reports

B.1–B.4 list existing litigation, one potential case, Police Officers Association labor negotiations, and price and terms for a potential purchase of Oak Valley property from Beaumont Unified School District. E.1 introduces Grant Analyst Jenny Espinosa. The agenda also includes public comment, Youth Council, legislative updates, City Manager and Council reports, and future agenda items.
