Executive summary

Council advanced infrastructure, public services, and local investment

Council approved the entire consent calendar, annexed Baldwin Ranch into two service districts, adopted a three-year transit plan and a capital-plan amendment, awarded two downtown business grants and three public-works contracts, and directed near- and long-term street-safety work. Stewart Park received clearance to restart construction.
