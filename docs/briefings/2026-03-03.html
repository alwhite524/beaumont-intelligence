Executive summary

What Council decided

Council advanced updates to regional transportation fees and subdivision rules, approved Tentative Tract Map 38926, increased the IOA Championship contribution to $40,000, and approved a consulting amendment. It received the billboard survey and City leasehold inventory and directed further work on public facilities and short-term-rental regulation.
