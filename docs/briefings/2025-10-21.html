Meeting outcomes and discussion

The meeting also included County youth-employment and business-workforce presentations, a Youth Council report, legislative and economic-development updates, and Council reports.

Closed-session report

Item B4 was removed without consideration. The public report-out stated there was no reportable action on B1–B3 and B5–B7.

G.1–G.12 · Consent calendar

The consent calendar was approved. Councilmember Fenn disclosed a proximity conflict on G.7; that item must not be described as a five-member unanimous vote. The transcript records the conflict separately from the roll call.

Watch discussion · 2:11:10 ↗

I.1 · Building standards

Council approved the first reading of the 2025 California Building Standards Code adoption with local amendments, including the amendments stated by staff. The transcript records five yes votes at 2:25:41. This was a first reading, not final adoption.

Watch discussion · 2:13:57 ↗

I.2 · Protective helmets

Council approved the first reading of a protective-helmet ordinance for human-powered and electrically assisted devices. The transcript records five yes votes at 2:33:58–2:34:35. Final ordinance language and subsequent adoption require the official ordinance record.

Watch discussion · 2:26:13 ↗

J.1–J.2 · Consulting agreements

Council considered J.1 and J.2 together and approved both with five yes votes. J.1 concerned property-tax consulting and audit services. The agenda identifies J.2 as the HdL master services agreement for sales-tax analysis, forecasting, and audit recovery services.

Watch discussion · 2:34:35 ↗

J.3 · EV charging idle fee

Council approved the recommended fee change with five yes votes. At 2:38:08–2:38:35, the discussion corrected the earlier presentation: the proposed idle fee was 25 cents per minute, not the stated $2 per hour. Confirm implementation details against the adopted resolution.

Watch discussion · 2:36:06 ↗

J.4 · Vacant and abandoned property

Council gave consensus direction to move forward with expanded vacant-property regulation. This was direction to prepare further action, not adoption of a new ordinance.

Watch discussion · 2:38:58 ↗

J.5 · Special-event permits

Council discussed event categories and permitting requirements and directed staff to return with an amended ordinance. The discussion included avoiding unnecessary permitting burdens for private gatherings.

Watch discussion · 2:42:30 ↗

J.6 · Governmental special events

Council approved an amended resolution concerning governmental-entity special-event exemptions. The transcript records five yes votes at 3:01:26–3:02:04. Consult the adopted resolution for the exact exemption and public-safety cost conditions.

Watch discussion · 2:52:14 ↗

J.7 · Billboard discussion scheduling

Council discussed a separate workshop and instead agreed to place the billboard discussion on the November 4 Council agenda.
