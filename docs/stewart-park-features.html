Later bid forecast and separate temporary skate procurement

The October records add a later Stewart Park bid forecast and related temporary skate amenities at Trevino Park. Trevino’s $120,000 authority is separate from Stewart Park’s P-10 phase estimates and ORLP grant; it does not prove Stewart Phase II was awarded or completed.

2026-10-20 · J.3 discussion

Later Stewart Park bid forecast

During the Downtown discussion, staff forecast a Stewart Park bid going out by the end of 2026 and returning early in 2027. This is later than July’s anticipated late-September Phase II advertising.

Evidence limit: Transcript-based staff forecast, not a Council approval, issued solicitation or award. The remark does not expressly identify the phase or package; obtain the bid documents before treating it as a revised formal Phase II schedule. July’s Phase I March 2027 completion forecast is not independently refreshed by this remark.

2026-10-06 · J.5 · 4–0

Temporary Trevino skate amenities authorized for competitive bidding

Council authorized competitive bidding, agreements and a purchase order up to $120,000. Funding is $72,609.98 in savings ($67,462.16 from P-09 and $5,147.82 from P-20) plus $47,390.02 from P27-01. The motion requires staff to bring back a budget adjustment reallocating the funding source. Voigt moved and White seconded.

Evidence limit: This is procurement authority, not a direct award to American Ramp or proof that a contract, installation, or funding-source adjustment has been completed.
