Featured question

What do Beaumont's Flock cameras collect, how long is it retained, and who can use it?

The cameras automatically photograph passing vehicles and license plates. They do not merely check a plate against a stolen-vehicle list: the system creates searchable records that can later be used to identify vehicles by plate and reported vehicle characteristics.

Verified from Beaumont's published policy
  • ALPR data is for official law-enforcement use.
  • Reasonable suspicion or probable cause is not required before an authorized user searches ALPR data.
  • Users must complete department-approved training.
  • Access is supposed to be protected by individual logins and recorded by name, date, and time.
  • The policy says audits should be conducted regularly.
  • Data may be shared with law-enforcement or prosecutorial agencies after a written, reviewed request.
  • The policy says server data should be retained for a minimum of one year, then purged unless it is evidence or subject to another lawful preservation requirement.
Critical evidence gap: The public-facing record does not disclose the retention period currently configured in Flock, publish access or sharing logs, identify every agency with direct or indirect access, publish audit frequency or results, or report substantiated misuse. A written policy is not proof that each safeguard was followed.

Direct answers

Common resident questions

Are the cameras used to issue traffic tickets?

At the August 20, 2024 meeting, police staff said they could not use the Flock cameras to cite drivers for conduct such as running stop signs because the image does not establish who was driving. That statement describes the department's represented use at that meeting; it is not a substitute for a binding restriction in the published ALPR policy.

Where are the cameras?

Staff said the 36 additions were selected around entrances and exits, areas associated with crime, and other strategic locations. The current public materials reviewed by Beaumont Intelligence do not provide a complete authoritative location list. A later traffic-signal planning report identifies some ALPR-equipped intersections but does not establish the entire network.

Does a search require a warrant?

The City's policy says reasonable suspicion or probable cause is not required for an authorized ALPR search. Whether a particular investigation later requires legal process depends on the facts and law; the public record does not show how often warrants, subpoenas, or other process are used.

Can other agencies access Beaumont data?

The policy allows sharing with law-enforcement and prosecutorial agencies for official purposes through a written request approved by the Administration Division Commander or designee. It does not publish a recipient list, and the available record does not establish whether other Flock-network customers have direct-search access.

Has the system been audited?

The policy says audits “should” occur regularly. Beaumont Intelligence has not located published ALPR audit reports, access-log reviews, error-rate reporting, complaint findings, or disciplinary outcomes. Those records are needed before compliance can be independently evaluated.

How effective is it?

Police officials gave examples involving burglaries, suspect vehicles, and stolen vehicles and said they had seen the cameras work. The reviewed public record does not provide enough aggregate data to calculate alerts, investigative leads, arrests, recoveries, false alerts, or cost per successful outcome.

What would make this record complete?

  • The current Flock configuration and actual retention setting.
  • A current camera inventory and installation-status record for the 36-camera expansion.
  • Agency-sharing settings, written requests, and recipient records, with lawful redactions where required.
  • User-access logs and completed audit reports.
  • Annual statistics for detections, searches, alerts, false positives, investigative uses, arrests, recoveries, complaints, and misuse findings.
  • The current contract or renewal record after the 2024 two-year term.
