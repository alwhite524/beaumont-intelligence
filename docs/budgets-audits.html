Plans and authority

Budgets

An adopted budget establishes the City’s financial plan and spending authority. It does not prove that projected revenue arrived or that an authorized amount was spent.

Completed results

Audits

An annual financial report presents year-end results and financial position after the fiscal year closes. Independent audit work provides stronger confirmation of actual results than an adopted plan or interim report.
