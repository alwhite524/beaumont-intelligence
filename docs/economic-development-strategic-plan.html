Beaumont’s 2026–2031 plan for local business growth, retail corridors, strategic sites, and investment.

The City contracted with TIP Strategies to update the plan. The steering committee staff report documents the role of the committee and stakeholder input.

The City announced adoption and published the plan. Its priorities cover business retention, target industries, retail corridors, development sites, quality of place, and marketing.
