Community Recreation Center Study

The feasibility study and business plan for a proposed recreation center, including public input and the funding authorized for the study.

Key records and decisions

August 18, 2026

Council unanimously approved a $176,945 professional services agreement with RHA Community Works and authorized changes up to five percent of the contract amount. Council also approved moving $150,000 of unspent recreation budget authority into FY 2026–2027 to fund part of the study.
