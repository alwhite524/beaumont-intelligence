Downtown Revitalization History

From business-focused zoning changes to long-range design and implementation decisions.

2022

Downtown zoning changes

Council approved amendments intended to reduce entry barriers, broaden selected downtown uses, and retain review for potentially incompatible uses. The amended motion passed 4–0.

Watch discussion · 2:02:27 ↗
Sept. 17, 2024

Elevate 2050 adopted

Council adopted the Downtown Beaumont Revitalization Plan as presented, 3–0. The discussion emphasized that adoption created a long-range framework; later milestones would return through public review and separate Council actions.

· Watch · 50:59 ↗ · Plan ↗
Aug. 19, 2025

Preliminary engineering directed

Council voted 3–2 to direct staff to move forward with six tasks covering investigation, analysis, preliminary design, reporting, and potential environmental work.

Watch · 2:22:28 ↗
Sept. 2, 2025

GHD agreement authorized

Council approved contract C25-120 by a 3–2 vote: $1,483,634 for a preliminary design report plus a $116,366 contingency, for total authority not to exceed $1.6 million. Council Members Martinez and Lara voted no after raising funding and project-priority concerns.

· Watch · 3:27:01 ↗
Nov. 18, 2025

Design CIP closed and contract terminated

Council unanimously amended the FY 2026 CIP, closing R25-15 and repurposing its budget toward Pennsylvania Avenue readiness. The City later reported that the GHD contract was terminated.

· Watch CIP item · 1:52:51 ↗
Jan. 20, 2026

Measured implementation directed

After reviewing project accounting and a high-level traffic analysis, Council directed staff toward smaller, financially measured steps. Discussion included wayfinding, a two- and five-year approach, focusing on a City-owned revitalization hub, and pursuing specific-plan funding.

· Watch · 1:39:05 ↗
2026

Business incentives and economic strategy

Council approved three downtown business incentives totaling $125,000 in stated forgivable-loan authority and adopted the 2026–2031 Economic Development Strategic Plan.
