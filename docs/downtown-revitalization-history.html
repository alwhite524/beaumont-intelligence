2022

Downtown zoning changes

Council approved amendments intended to reduce entry barriers, broaden selected downtown uses, and retain review for potentially incompatible uses. The amended motion passed 4–0.

Sept. 17, 2024

Elevate 2050 adopted

Council adopted the Downtown Beaumont Revitalization Plan as presented, 3–0. Council Member Voigt moved, Mayor Fenn seconded, and Council Members White and Lara were absent.

Adopted minutes ↗ · Plan ↗
Aug. 19, 2025

Preliminary engineering directed

Council voted 3–2 to direct staff to move forward with six tasks covering investigation, analysis, preliminary design, reporting, and potential environmental work.

Watch · 2:22:28 ↗
Sept. 2, 2025

GHD agreement authorized

Council unanimously approved contract C25-120: $1,483,634 for a preliminary design report plus a $116,366 contingency, for total authority not to exceed $1.6 million.

Official meeting record ↗
Nov. 18, 2025

Design CIP closed and contract terminated

Council amended the FY 2026 CIP, closed R25-15, and repurposed its budget for the Pennsylvania Avenue Grade Separation. The City later reported that the GHD contract was terminated.

Jan. 20, 2026

Short-term goals directed

After reviewing the plan history and a high-level traffic analysis, Council directed staff to develop short-term goals toward the larger downtown revitalization plan.

2026

Business incentives and economic strategy

Council approved three downtown business incentives totaling $125,000 in stated forgivable-loan authority and adopted the 2026–2031 Economic Development Strategic Plan.
