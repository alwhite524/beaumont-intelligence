Trevino Park Temporary Skate Amenities

The temporary skate installation proposed for one of Trevino Park’s basketball courts during Stewart Park renovation.

Key records and decisions

October 6, 2026 · Competitive procurement

Council voted 4–0 to authorize competitive bidding and agreements up to $120,000. Staff proposed bolt-down skate features on one basketball court, leaving the second court available for basketball. The motion authorized a competitive procurement.

Council decision and minutes → · Staff report and proposal →

October 6, 2026 · Funding direction

The motion identified $67,462.16 in P-09 savings, $5,147.82 in P-20 savings, and $47,390.02 from Trevino Park project P27-01. Council directed staff to return with a budget adjustment to reallocate the funding. This authorization is tracked separately from Stewart Park construction.
