Beaumont Transit Yard

CIP T25-01 is a planned transit operations and maintenance facility at Fourth Street and Veile Avenue, with fleet parking, fueling, and charging.

Key records and decisions

July 2024 to June 2025 · Design

Council awarded the design contract to Gillis + Panichapan Architects in July 2024. The firm presented a design update in June 2025. The City’s project page documents review of 30% plans for the approximately 6.15-acre site.

City project and design history →

July 21, 2026 · Capital plan amendment

The amended capital plan listed a $24 million estimated project cost, $16,138,574 in programmed funding, and a $7,861,426 funding gap for T25-01. The August staff report described $16,138,574 as the project budget. These figures describe different measures.

July capital plan amendment → · Capital Improvement Plan →

August 18, 2026 · Environmental decision

Council unanimously adopted the Mitigated Negative Declaration and Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program. The mitigation program identifies measures the City must track as the project moves forward.
