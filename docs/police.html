Featured public question

What do Beaumont's Flock cameras collect—and who can use the data?

Automated license plate readers · Privacy · Oversight

Flock cameras photograph passing vehicles and license plates so police can search for vehicles connected to crimes or receive alerts involving wanted or stolen vehicles. The City's published policy permits official law-enforcement use without requiring reasonable suspicion before a search, calls for logged access and regular audits, and allows approved sharing with law-enforcement or prosecutorial agencies.

What the public record does not yet answer: Beaumont has not published audit results, aggregate search and alert counts, misuse findings, a complete agency-sharing list, or a reconciliation between the City's minimum-one-year policy language and the retention period actually configured in the Flock system.
