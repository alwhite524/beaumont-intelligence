Project Timeline 
The following is a detailed timeline for project milestones and important submittals: 
 December 19, 2017 – Contract award to Moffatt Nichol (M&N) for environmental 
services for 3 projects (Widening, Grade Separation, and Interchange) 
 June 19, 2018 – Contract Award to IDC for 35% level design 
 August 28, 2018 – Amendment #1 to IDC for additional surveying 
 January 2, 2019 – Amendment #1 to M&N for additional regulatory permitting 
services 
 May 2019 – IDC submitted final 35% plans to the City.  They were not reviewed 
or approved by Caltrans or UPRR.  Design was then paused until July 2022.  
 February 2, 2021 – Amendment #2 for M&N for Penn Widening due to updated 
environmental regulations.  
 July 19, 2022 – IDC Amendment #2 for final design based on the 
understanding that the 35% design was complete. 
 August 2022 – CEQA NOE filed with the County of Riverside 
 November 15, 2022 – Amendment #3 for M&N to add NEPA 
 January 23, 2023 – UPRR Bridge Concept Plan Submittal #1 to UPRR 
 February 16, 2023 – UPRR Type Selection Submittal #1 
 April 10, 2023 – UPRR Type Selection Submittal #2 
 June 11, 2023 – UPRR Type Selection Submittal #3 
 December 5, 2023 – Amendment #4 for M&N for contract extension 
 December 22, 2023 – UPRR Bridge Concept Plan Submittal #2 to UPRR 
 January 29, 2024 – UPRR Bridge Concept Plan Submittal #3 to UPRR 
 January 22, 2024 – Track and Shoofly Design Submittal to UPRR (preliminary) 
 February 28, 2024 – GAD and DSDD Submittal #1 to Caltrans 
 April 8, 2024 – Track and Shoofly Design Submittal to UPRR (10%) 
 April 8, 2024 – UPRR Type Selection Submittal #4 
 May 1, 2024 – GAD and DSDD Submittal #2 to Caltrans
