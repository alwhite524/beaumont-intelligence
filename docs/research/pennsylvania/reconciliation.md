# Pennsylvania Avenue cost and schedule reconciliation

Research date: October 24, 2026. Project: Beaumont CIP 2017-012; CTC PPNO 3025G; Caltrans EA 1P300 / PN 0823000158. This is an internal evidence memorandum, not an agency-certified reconciliation.

## Findings

The latest collected City estimate is **$147,559,817**, not $140 million. No collected post-withdrawal record establishes a replacement construction start or completion date. June 2027 was an allocation/readiness deadline under the former award, not a construction opening date. The August 4 Council outcome in the existing meeting record authorizes withdrawal and reapplication; it does not establish a new award. The October 20 mayoral report is transcript evidence of a reported grant return, not the CTC disposition itself.

### Funding arithmetic: an internal inconsistency

August staff report p.3 and RCTC slide 4 list $49.4M TCEP, $7.225M STBG, $2M federal earmark, $5M SB125, $3.15M UPRR, $6.698M RTIP, $12,548,093 DIF, and $5.82M General Fund. These sum to **$91,841,093**.

The staff report p.5 instead shows **$92,841,093** current budget and uses **$50.4M TCEP** in its chart. The extra $1M exactly accounts for the difference. Earlier project records distinguish $49.4M for grade separation from $1M for a separate charging project; the August chart alone cannot establish that the latter is available to this project.

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| $147,559,817 minus p.5 budget $92,841,093 | $54,718,724 chart gap |
| $147,559,817 minus p.3 funding $91,841,093 | $55,718,724 gap using funding table |
| p.3 funding minus withdrawn $49.4M | $42,441,093 nominal remaining sources |
| $147,559,817 minus $42,441,093 | **$105,118,724 nominal post-withdrawal gap** |

The roughly $110M need reported in the meeting is not reproduced by either table. It may reflect eligibility, source availability, rounding, or a different scope, but those explanations remain unverified. The nominal remainder is not certified cash on hand or unspent construction funding. The p.5 paid-to-date total is $3,248,756; spending is distinct from authorization and remaining funding. Pending RCE, CRISI, INFRA, and TCEP applications must not be counted as awards or added together as secured resources.

### Schedule crosswalk

* The retained fact sheet's November 2025 start / October 2027 completion is historical.
* The May CTC item is an allocation-extension request to June 30, 2027. Its recommendation alone is not the adopted vote; the August staff report p.2 independently describes the extension as previously approved.
* The June announcement describes June 2027 Ready-to-List, not completion.
* August RCTC slides 22–24 describe April 2027 readiness ahead of June allocation, a best-case December 30, 2026 final-design milestone, and a December 2027 construction award. Slide 8 rounds final design to January 2027. These are planning targets under the former funding path, not achieved milestones.
* August staff report p.4 anticipates September 2026 95% design. No subsequent acceptance record was collected, so do not label that milestone complete merely because its target date passed.
* Slides 18–19 and 27–28 identify UPRR agreement timing, utility/access work, and prior MDP Line 2 construction as constraints; some risks carry 12–18-month potential delays. Environmental clearance does not establish completion of every construction permit.
* Withdrawal/reapplication removes the basis for presenting the former deadline as a current promised start. Staff also explicitly says development continues on the existing schedule: withdrawal does not prove all engineering targets were abandoned or work stopped.
