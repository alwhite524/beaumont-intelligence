Automated license plate readers

Flock cameras: operational record and limits

Documented since 2020

What the system does

Fixed cameras capture vehicle images and license plates. Authorized users can search records during investigations and receive information involving stolen, wanted, or otherwise identified vehicles.

What Council funded

In 2024 Council approved a two-year subscription for 36 additional cameras at $225,900. The subscription included cameras, mounting, service, maintenance, and acquired data.

What remains unmeasured

No reviewed public report provides annual searches, alerts, false positives, arrests, recoveries, complaint findings, access audits, or cost per successful investigative outcome.

Do not conflate systems: Flock ALPR cameras, City CCTV cameras, body-worn cameras, Peregrine investigative software, and the Flock drone program collect different information under different agreements and operational rules.
