Recent spending and contract authorizations

September and October Council actions add contract and purchasing authority to the adopted budget. They do not establish payments or completed projects. September 15 and October 6 findings were checked against supplied City minutes; October 20 findings remain transcript-based pending approved minutes.

September 15: staffing agreements and signal award

Council authorized employment agreements for Finance Director Travis Hickey and Deputy City Manager Kari Mendoza, and awarded the Oak Valley Parkway/Palm Avenue signal work for $847,090 plus $84,710 contingency, totaling $931,800.

Minutes review and supporting records →

October 6: contract capacity and equipment

Dekra-Lite’s aggregate ceiling rose from $260,000 to $500,000 within the approved budget, with up to 5% amendment/change-order authority; LSL accounting increased from $65,000 to $165,000. Highland Springs smart-signal work was awarded at $795,869 including contingency.

Park equipment purchases total $972,933.56, with maximum combined authority of $1,021,580.23. The final motion uses P-04 savings for the playground purchase and contingency. Trevino skate amenities received competitive procurement authority up to $120,000, with a funding-source budget adjustment required to return.

Minutes review and funding-source corrections →

October 20: phase limits and corrected amounts

Police-station Phase I authority is $241,727 including contingency within a $5,437,851 overall agreement ceiling subject to later phase funding and authorization. The roughly $88.5 million project estimate is not a construction award or full funding commitment.

Council approved IOA sponsorship up to $80,000 and directed downtown Phase I design; the corrected combined STB design figure is $68,850, with construction funding to return. The Tyler three-year amendment was corrected to $631,892.87; estimated variable wastewater card fees of $346,144.50 are separate. Staff requested no additional appropriation for Tyler.

G.1 warrants were approved separately after discussion of missing Treasurer signatures; later ratification is not established.
