2026-10-06 · J.5 · 4–0

Temporary Trevino skate amenities authorized for competitive bidding

Council authorized competitive bidding, agreements and a purchase order up to $120,000. Funding is $72,609.98 in savings ($67,462.16 from P-09 and $5,147.82 from P-20) plus $47,390.02 from P27-01. The motion requires staff to bring back a budget adjustment reallocating the funding source. Voigt moved and White seconded.

Evidence limit: This is procurement authority, not a direct award to American Ramp or proof that a contract, installation, or funding-source adjustment has been completed.
