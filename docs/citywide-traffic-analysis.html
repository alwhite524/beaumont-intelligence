Citywide Traffic Analysis

The scope, funding, and Council authorization for the Fehr & Peers citywide traffic study, CIP R27-06.

Key records and decisions

2026 capital plan · Study funding

The capital plan lists $300,000 in programmed funding for R27-06. The October staff report identifies traffic-signal development impact fees as the funding source and references FY 2027–2028.

October 20, 2026 · Purchase order authorization

Council unanimously approved the consent calendar, including authority to issue a revised $212,940 purchase order to Fehr & Peers. The proposal prices the main study at $150,220 and two optional tasks for preliminary design and signing or striping at a combined $62,720.
