Planning and preliminary engineering

Council authorized GHD contract C25-120 by a 3–2 vote in September 2025: $1,483,634 for a preliminary design report plus a $116,366 contingency, for a $1.6 million ceiling. In November, Council closed CIP R25-15 and redirected its budget to Pennsylvania Avenue; the City later reported that the GHD contract was terminated. Authorization, payments before termination, and remaining balances must be tracked separately.

Verified spending snapshot

At the January 20, 2026 meeting, staff reported approximately $5.5 million spent: about $2.49 million for real estate, $900,000 for Market Nights, $89,000 for incentives, $500,000 for planning and design, and $1.5 million for public improvements. These were rounded meeting figures, not an audited project ledger.

Business incentives

Three verified 2026 awards total $125,000 in stated forgivable-loan authority. Actual disbursement and future forgiveness should be tracked separately.

Future construction

Staff gave an approximately $27 million early estimate for roadway, sidewalk, lighting, signage, and related public-right-of-way improvements, while cautioning that scope and market conditions were not defined. Staff stated that no funding was then identified. Buildings, façades, and incentive programs were outside that estimate.

Next financial record needed: a project-level accounting that reconciles CD03, R25-15, property acquisition and rehabilitation, lighting and streetscape work, business-program funding, transfers, contracts, expenditures, and remaining balances.
