Decisions and planning discussions

These dated findings use the linked minutes and, where available, supplied transcripts. Transcript timestamps are navigation aids; automatic transcription can misidentify names and numbers. Video playback has not been independently checked. Proposals and forecasts remain distinct from adopted budgets, payments and completed work.

2026-05-21 · Item C.1

Second budget workshop: sponsorship direction

The minutes record direction to add $45,000 to Council sponsorships and return with a Donation and Sponsorship Policy. The transcript records staff direction to consolidate donations and sponsorships into one $273,300 line rather than earmark individual events.

Evidence limit: This was budget preparation, not final budget adoption or approval of individual sponsorship payments.

2026-04-28 · Item C.1

Forecast alternatives before budget adoption

Council requested another workshop, different forecasting scenarios, a review of sponsorships and a future discussion of Council benefits. Staff's presentation modeled a proposed $75 million police-station bond with debt service beginning in FY2030.

Evidence limit: The bond was a forecast assumption, not an issuance authorization. Obtain the presentation and assumptions before reproducing scenario totals.

2025-06-17 · Item J.3

Revenue outlook and phase-two direction

Council received and filed GrassrootsLab's Revenue Outlook and Review of Options report and directed staff to proceed with phase two. The minutes record a 3-0 vote.

Evidence limit: The report and packet are still needed to establish the detailed risks, options and phase-two scope. This action did not itself enact a tax.

2025-06-17 · Item J.4–J.6

Fire-service and pavement funding decisions

The minutes record separate 4-0 approvals of the Fire Station 106 equipment/staffing cost-share agreement, rescission of the $1,383,753 fire-engine purchase order in favor of including the engine in the County engine-use agreement, and Pavement Management Program Scenario 1 aligned with the adopted FY2026–30 CIP.

Evidence limit: The rescinded purchase order is not a verified saving of the same amount. Executed agreements, engine-use costs and pavement schedules remain to be collected.

Minutes · Full meeting video

2025-02-04 · Item J.4

Midyear budget: amendments and timing effects

Council unanimously received and filed the FY2024–25 midyear report and approved its amendments. The transcript explains that a one-time sales-tax audit correction affected receipts and that delayed County fire-service invoices affected reported expenditure timing. Council requested a list of authorized but unfilled positions.

Evidence limit: Interim receipts and delayed payments do not establish recurring revenue growth or permanent savings. Amendment schedules and audited actuals are needed for reconciliation.
