The establishment and evolution of PLUS

Read chronologically from the early mitigation discussions to the latest policy version located. Committee reports and staff direction are labeled separately from adoption.

2022-01-18 · 19

Logistics impacts and a land-use committee discussed

Background and staff direction

During the Development Impact Fee discussion, Mayor White proposed an ad hoc committee with Martinez and Fenn to examine logistics growth, traffic impacts and mitigation. The recorded unanimous motion directed preparation of impact-fee nexus-study RFPs, budget inclusion and a future DIF agenda item.

Evidence limit: The committee discussion is documented in the transcript. The minutes' motion concerns the DIF program; this was not adoption of PLUS or a logistics fee.

2022-04-05 · Council reports

Land-use mitigation committee reports outreach

Committee report

Fenn reported that he and Martinez had met with development-industry representatives, discussed future warehouse projects and emphasized the City's General Plan. He described an invitation to discuss project ideas before developers committed time and money.

Evidence limit: This is a Council report in the supplied transcript, not a policy adoption, entitlement approval or waiver of development review.

Interactive agenda · Supplied transcript · Video · 04:50:58

2022-09-06 · 13

Council establishes and names PLUS

Adopted · unanimous motions

Council unanimously accepted redlines to the draft land-use policy for logistics, warehouse, distribution and similar industrial development with a 1,000-foot setback stipulation. Fenn moved and Martinez seconded. A separate unanimous motion by Lara, seconded by White, named the policy PLUS.

Evidence limit: The October clarification identifies September 6 as the adoption date. These minutes establish the motions; the original adopted policy is still needed for a complete version comparison.

Official minutes

2022-10-04 · 11

Applicability clarified after adoption

Clarification adopted · unanimous

On Lara's motion, seconded by Martinez, Council unanimously clarified the policy's applicability concerning formal entitlement and pre-applications before the September 6, 2022 adoption date.

Evidence limit: The minutes use the wording 'applicable upon formal entitlement, not including pre-applications prior to adoption date.' Project-specific applicability requires the policy version and the application record.

Official minutes

2023-12-19 · J.7

Community Benefit Fee language sent for amendment

Staff direction

Council directed staff to amend PLUS language as presented following discussion of formalizing the Community Benefit Fee.

Evidence limit: The minutes record direction to staff, not adoption of the later amended policy, a fee collection or an expenditure.

Official minutes

2024-02-06 · J.3

Community-benefit and project requirements amended

Amendments adopted · unanimous

Council unanimously adopted the updated PLUS as presented on White's motion, seconded by Fenn. The item described formalizing the Community Benefit Fee and updating Siting and Design, Operations and Construction Phase requirements.

Evidence limit: Adoption does not establish the amount collected, the balance available or how community-benefit funds were spent.

Official minutes

2024-05-21 · J.3

Further PLUS revisions adopted

Amendments adopted · unanimous

Council unanimously adopted amended PLUS as proposed on White's motion, seconded by Voigt. The item described Community Benefit Fee provisions and changes to Analysis, Construction Phase, Siting and Design, and Operations.

Evidence limit: The policy amendment is distinct from Beaumont Pointe entitlements considered at the same meeting. Project-specific benefit terms should not be treated as a universal PLUS fee schedule.

Official minutes · City agenda

2025-03-04 · G.9

AB 98 revisions adopted

Amendments adopted · unanimous

Council unanimously adopted PLAN2024-0134, the amended PLUS presented for compliance with Assembly Bill 98, on Lara's motion, seconded by White. The policy linked from the City's Planning page carries this date.

Evidence limit: This is the latest policy version located for this center. It does not by itself establish subsequent enforcement, project compliance or the current status of state law.
