# Business Items

## Pennsylvania Avenue Grade Separation Project

### Subject

Trade Corridor Enhancement Program (TEP)

### Staff Recommendation

Withdraw from the 2024 TEP funding cycle and immediately pursue the 2026 cycle.

### Reasons Presented

- Ready-to-List deadline risk
- Utility relocation challenges
- Union Pacific coordination
- Right-of-way acquisition
- Caltrans coordination
- Master Drainage Line 2 dependency
- Inflation and construction cost escalation

### Funding Status

- Approximately **$91.8 million** secured.
- Estimated remaining funding gap approximately **$110 million**.

### Council Action

Council authorized staff to:

- Withdraw the current TEP application.
- Reapply during the 2026 funding cycle.
- Continue project development.
- Continue grant applications.
- Continue design advancement.
- Continue right-of-way work.

**Vote**
Unanimous.
