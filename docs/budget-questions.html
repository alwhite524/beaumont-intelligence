Does an adopted budget show what was spent?

No. It establishes a spending plan and legal authority. Interim reports and the later audited financial statements show actual activity at different stages.

Why can year-end figures change?

Fourth-quarter reports can precede final closing entries, reconciliations, and audit adjustments. The ACFR is the stronger source for final prior-year results.

Is the Capital Improvement Plan a construction invoice?

No. The CIP is a multi-year planning document. Projects, timing, estimates, and funding can change through amendments and later contracts.

Does a contract authorization prove payment?

No. Authorization permits an agreement up to stated terms. Actual invoices, disbursements, and accounting records are needed to establish payment.

Does a grant award mean cash has been received?

Not necessarily. Awards may require an agreement, eligible expenditures, matching funds, milestones, or reimbursement before funds are received.
