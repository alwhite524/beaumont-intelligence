Beaumont Nights

The downtown market-night program, its reported 2026 activity, and Council direction for the 2027 season.

Key records and decisions

April to July 2026 · Towncenter series

The City scheduled Thursday market nights on Beaumont Avenue between Sixth and Eighth streets, with local vendors, food, and live entertainment.

City program and vendor information →

September 1, 2026 · Council direction

Council voted 5–0 to receive the 2026 recap, direct staff to develop a proposed 15-date 2027 calendar, and approve a designated beer garden subject to licensing and responsible-service requirements. Staff estimated 33,000 area visits across 12 events. The estimate counts visits and may include repeat visitors.

Council decision and program recap →

October 6, 2026 · Bicycle Day

Council voted 4–0 to co-host Stage 2 of the 2027 Redlands Bicycle Classic on April 8, 2027, with youth races and a bicycle-themed Beaumont Nights event. The motion authorized City resources and work on the event agreement.

The final 2027 calendar and budget, vendor information, alcohol licensing, the Bicycle Day agreement, and post-event reporting.
