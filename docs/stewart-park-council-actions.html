2022-01-18 · Item 17

Stewart Park design RFP authorized

Council reached consensus to approve the draft design RFP for Stewart Park and direct staff to publish it.

Evidence limit: This authorized solicitation; it was not a design contract award or construction approval.

2022-03-01 · Item 15

Review group for Stewart Park proposals

Council designated the City Treasurer and a Finance and Audit Committee subcommittee to review Stewart Park design proposals and other RFPs, except Public Works bids requiring the lowest responsive bidder.

Evidence limit: The outcome differs from the agenda proposal to appoint a Council member; no design award was made by this item.
