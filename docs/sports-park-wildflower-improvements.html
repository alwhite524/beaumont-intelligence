Sports Park and Wildflower Park Improvements

The Sports Park field expansion and a later joint equipment purchase for Sports Park and Wildflower Park.

Key records and decisions

August 2025 · Sports Park construction

Council awarded Zeco, Inc. a public works contract for Sports Park construction. The City’s project summary describes work on playing fields and field lighting.

City renovation and construction history →

September 2026 · Field improvements

The City announced the enhanced Sports Park, highlighting new artificial turf fields and improvements to lighting, drainage, irrigation, and walkways.

City Sports Park improvements announcement →

October 6, 2026 · Equipment purchase

Council voted 4–0 to approve $972,933.56 in combined playground and field-lighting equipment purchases, with a maximum purchase authority of $1,021,580.23. The final motion directed the City to use remaining CIP P-04 savings for the playground equipment and its contingency.
