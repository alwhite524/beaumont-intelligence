Current answers

What can be answered now

Answers link to the center page where the supporting record is organized.

01

Is the interchange under construction?

Yes. The documented transition to construction followed the April 28, 2026 groundbreaking. Opening and final acceptance remain future milestones. Construction record →

02

What is Phase II building?

The documented scope includes six freeway ramps, a partial-cloverleaf configuration, a widened Potrero crossing, and related local-road and infrastructure work. Design record →

03

How much does the project cost?

Official sources use different figures for total project cost, construction investment, and the construction award. Those measures are reconciled without treating them as interchangeable. Funding record →

04

Who is responsible for delivery?

RCTC, Beaumont, Caltrans, the California Transportation Commission, WRCOG, and Ames Construction have distinct funding, approval, maintenance, and delivery roles. Public actions →

05

Will it reduce congestion?

Congestion relief, safety, freight movement, and improved access are stated project purposes. They are not measured outcomes yet and should be evaluated after opening. Impact framework →

06

Is the concept plan the final design?

No. The January 2019 image explains the intended layout but is labeled as a historical concept, not a final or as-built drawing. Inspect concept plan →

Open record

What still needs verification

Historical gap

March 17, 2015 progress report

The Council agenda identifies a Potrero Interchange Progress Report, but the staff report, attachments, minutes, and any resulting direction have not been incorporated into the evidence set.

Contract gap

Complete executed contract chain

The center documents major public actions, but it does not yet present a complete contract, amendment, change-order, payment, and closeout chain in one register.

Delivery gap

Cost and schedule performance

Track notices to proceed, monthly progress, approved change orders, delay notices, payment totals, substantial completion, final acceptance, and the eventual opening date.

Outcome gap

Post-opening performance

Traffic volumes, travel times, collision trends, freight routing, emergency access, and surrounding-road effects will be needed to test the project's stated benefits.

Current research rule: Unknowns remain visible. They are not converted into conclusions merely because construction has begun.
