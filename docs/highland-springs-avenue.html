Highland Springs Avenue Improvements

Council decisions and regional plans for traffic signals, coordinated street work, and the Interstate 10 interchange on Highland Springs Avenue.

Key records and decisions

October 6, 2026 · Smart-signal construction

Council voted 4–0 to award California Professional Engineering the R26-05 smart-signal contract for $723,517, with a $72,352 contingency. The $795,869 authorization covers a project to upgrade signals at 11 Highland Springs Avenue intersections.

Council award and minutes review → · Scope and bidding records →

October 20, 2026 · Banning street-work agreement

Beaumont Council approved the cooperative agreement for Highland Springs Avenue and Ramsey–Sixth Street work within street rehabilitation project R26-01. Staff estimated Banning’s share at $369,580 with contingency. Under the agreement, Beaumont would lead the work and seek reimbursement for actual costs attributable to Banning.

Council decision and minutes → · Agreement and cost estimate → · Citywide Street Rehabilitation →

Regional project · Interstate 10 interchange

The Riverside County Transportation Commission, Caltrans, Banning, and Beaumont are advancing a separate interchange project. The proposed work would reconfigure lanes beneath I-10 and add ramp merging lanes.
