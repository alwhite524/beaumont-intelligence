What Changed?

From concept to a documented, phased project

Project Definition

  • Community input and design concepts were assembled.
  • Phase I and Phase II features were identified.
  • Construction and consultant records became part of the public archive.

Funding and Compliance

  • Phase I is described by the City as fully funded.
  • A $2.5 million Phase II award was preserved.
  • Environmental review and pre-award requirements were documented.

From the Council archive

Historical findings from the collected 2022 and 2025 meeting packets. These decisions describe the record at the time, not current project status.

2025-06-03 · Item J.11

Stewart Park construction pause approved

Council unanimously selected Option 1 to pause all construction activity effective June 4 until the grant agreement was executed, as recorded in the minutes following the ORLP update.

Evidence limit: This is a historical pause decision. It does not establish the eventual restart date, final grant reimbursement or current construction status.

2022-04-05 · Item 6

PlaceWorks design agreement awarded

Council approved 4-0 a PlaceWorks agreement for Stewart Park landscape architecture and engineering design services, not to exceed $647,971. Martinez was absent.

Evidence limit: This is a design-services authorization, not a construction award, payment total or completed improvement.

Review decision and evidence →
2022-03-01 · Item 15

Review group for Stewart Park proposals

Council designated the City Treasurer and a Finance and Audit Committee subcommittee to review Stewart Park design proposals and other RFPs, except Public Works bids requiring the lowest responsive bidder.

Evidence limit: The outcome differs from the agenda proposal to appoint a Council member; no design award was made by this item.

Review decision and evidence →
2022-01-18 · Item 17

Stewart Park design RFP authorized

Council reached consensus to approve the draft design RFP for Stewart Park and direct staff to publish it.

Evidence limit: This authorized solicitation; it was not a design contract award or construction approval.
