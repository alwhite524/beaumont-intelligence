2024-10-01 · Items J.2

Pennsylvania design agreement: new scope and remaining authority

Approved unanimously

Council awarded IDC an amended and restated agreement not to exceed $1,884,482. The staff report separates $1,209,191 of additional scope from $675,291 remaining on the prior contract; the cumulative agreement history totals $3,134,124. These are different accounting measures, not three new awards.

Evidence limit / next records: The report targeted Ready to List in September 2025 and reported bridge design at 60%, track at 25% and civil at 50%. These are October 2024 statements, not verified current progress or a construction start. Executed agreement, later schedule and payments remain separate checks.
