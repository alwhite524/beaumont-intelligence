2020

Flock begins in Sundance

A 2023 City staff report says a July 29, 2020 data-sharing agreement limited Flock installations to public right-of-way within the Sundance HOA. The Police Department's 2020 annual report also identified automated license plate readers as an operating technology.

Review the Flock record →
2022

Existing body-camera platform retained

Council approved a new four-year Intrensic agreement after staff described the existing cloud evidence, replacement of 50 cameras, and acquisition of 20 additional cameras. This record provides context for the later Axon transition.

2022

Flock retention legislation debated

During an April legislative update, the police chief opposed a proposed requirement to erase unrelated ALPR data after 24 hours, arguing that delayed crime reports could make earlier vehicle records necessary.

Read transcript →
2023

Flock installation authority expands citywide

Council approved a ten-year encroachment agreement allowing Flock equipment in public rights-of-way throughout Beaumont. The consent-calendar action passed 5–0.

Read transcript →
2024

Future station estimate discussed

An October capital-project discussion compared an approximately $71.8 million planning estimate with a then-published $50 million CIP construction amount. Neither figure proves final cost or available funding.

Watch discussion · 2:01:20 ↗
2024

Flock network expands from 11 cameras

Police staff reported 11 existing cameras. Council then approved a two-year, $225,900 subscription adding 36 cameras, for an implied total of 47 after deployment.

Watch discussion · 4:08:21 ↗
2025

Station planning and Axon agreement

Council formed a station-planning subcommittee in August. In December, it approved a five-year Axon body-camera and evidence-technology agreement 5–0.

Review station history →
2026

SRO, Peregrine, and station priority actions

Council approved the three-officer school agreement, approved Peregrine investigative software, and placed the future Police Department Station second in its revised strategic priority order.
