2022

Existing body-camera platform retained

Council approved a new four-year Intrensic agreement after staff described the existing cloud evidence, replacement of 50 cameras, and acquisition of 20 additional cameras. This record provides context for the later Axon transition.

Future station estimate discussed

An October capital-project discussion compared an approximately $71.8 million planning estimate with a then-published $50 million CIP construction amount. Neither figure proves final cost or available funding.

Station planning and Axon agreement

Council formed a station-planning subcommittee in August. In December, it approved a five-year Axon body-camera and evidence-technology agreement 5–0.
