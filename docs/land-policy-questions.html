Questions about PLUS

When was PLUS established?

September 6, 2022. Council accepted the draft redlines with a setback stipulation and separately voted to name it PLUS. The October 4 clarification identifies September 6 as the adoption date.

Read the adoption evidence →

Did the early discussions adopt a policy?

The January 2022 motion directed DIF study preparation. The April report described committee outreach. PLUS adoption followed in September.

Read the early history →

When did community-benefit language change?

Staff direction in December 2023 preceded adopted amendments in February and May 2024. These actions do not establish actual receipts or expenditures.

Review the amendment sequence →

What policy document is available?

The City Planning page links a PLUS document dated March 4, 2025, the date of Council's AB 98 amendment.

Open the policy source →

Does PLUS replace environmental review?

The located policy describes project review alongside CEQA and project-level mitigation.

Read the policy →

Has the policy worked?

The adoption record alone cannot answer that. Project conditions, inspection records, complaints, enforcement and measured impacts are needed to evaluate implementation.
