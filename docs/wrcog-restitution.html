About this center

Documenting what was ordered, recovered, and retained

The evidence layer supporting My Story and independent public review.

This Center documents the WRCOG litigation and settlement, criminal restitution, and other recoveries involving the City of Beaumont. It is the documentary research layer that My Story can cite.

Settlement approval text reviewed; collections unverified. The May 16, 2017 packet documents the proposed amended agreement terms, and the minutes document Council approval. A fully executed agreement and records of actual receipts remain needed. An empty ledger means records have not been loaded; it does not mean no money was recovered.
Recovery framework

Keep the financial tracks separate

  1. WRCOG judgment and settlement: exposure and the terms resolving it.
  2. Criminal restitution orders: amounts a court ordered.
  3. Restitution collected: documented payments actually received.
  4. Civil litigation recoveries: separately documented civil settlements.
  5. Insurance and professional-liability settlements.
  6. Other recoveries or reimbursements, including separately classified loan repayments.
  7. WRCOG’s contractual share of qualifying recoveries.
  8. Beaumont’s net recovery after documented allocations and costs.

Ordered does not mean paid. Gross recovery does not mean Beaumont’s net recovery. An unknown amount will remain unknown, never silently become zero.

Guarantee and legal fees

Section 1.3.6: the City guarantees WRCOG recovery of $7 million from Third Party Claims, plus a fee share equal to the lesser of 50% of legal fees incurred pursuing those claims or $1 million. WRCOG advances that fee share; reimbursement forms part of the Litigation Guaranty.

Packet pages 164; Item 3 extract pages 20

Conditional repayment

If recoveries had not reached $8 million by July 1, 2022, the text provides for annual payments beginning August 1, 2022 over 20 years, adjusted for subsequent recoveries. This records a conditional provision, not a finding that payments became due or were made. Section 1.3.7 addresses indemnity and the guarantee cap.

Packet pages 164–165; Item 3 extract pages 20–21

Claims and assignment

Defined Third Party Claims include certain restitution claims as well as other claims. Assignment is upon written request from WRCOG; the packet does not establish that every criminal restitution payment was credited under this provision.

Packet pages 162–164; Item 3 extract pages 18–20

Other obligations remain separate

The text separately addresses Measure A revenue sharing capped at $9.4 million, including an $8 million recovery condition; a $2 million payment to WRCOG; $2.1 million for Cherry Valley Boulevard/I-10 design; a $3 million designated Pennsylvania Avenue account; and transportation improvements. These obligations are not recovery receipts.

Packet pages 163; Item 3 extract pages 19

Judgment recital is not the later balance

The recitals state $42,994,879 principal, $14,809,096 prejudgment interest, and $2,077,525 fees/costs, totaling $59,881,500, with further interest described at $8,246 per day. This does not by itself verify the later approximately $62 million figure.

Packet pages 160; Item 3 extract pages 16

Release language and execution

Section 2 contains releases with exceptions for Third Party Claims. The agreement states effectiveness requires full execution and governing-body approval; signature lines in this approval copy are blank. Actual execution and court dismissal require further records.

Packet pages 160, 165–166, 171–173; Item 3 extract pages 16, 21–22, 27–29

Original April agreement and May amendment

The April approval copy names the City and WRCOG and contains the same recovery brackets and guarantee in the reviewed clauses. The May staff report describes adding City-related entities and assignment upon WRCOG written request. The April packet also supplies Exhibits 1–6, inventoried but not fully analyzed. Both packet copies have blank signature lines; this is a limited comparison, not verification of execution.
