FY 2024 plan

Earlier site and design concept

The adopted FY 2024 budget programmed $1.5 million for design and described an earlier concept off Potrero Boulevard, adjacent to the West Side Fire Station. That historical concept should not be treated as the current site decision.

2024 discussion

Planning estimates compared

Council discussed an approximately $71.8 million conceptual estimate and a $50 million construction amount in the then-current CIP. The discussion identified a discrepancy, not a final bid or expenditure.
