New Police Station

The funding, program management, and delivery decisions for Beaumont’s new police station, CIP PS-07.

Key records and decisions

June 2026 · Capital plan

The June capital plan listed an $80 million project estimate, $8,462,372 in programmed funding, and $71,537,628 unfunded. The October staff estimate later rose to $88,475,000, leaving an $80,012,628 difference against the same identified funding.

Compare the June and October funding records →

October 20, 2026 · Phase I program management

Council unanimously approved a Tilden-Coil program-management agreement with a $5,437,851 ceiling subject to later phase funding and Council authorization. The action funded Phase I services at $219,752 plus $21,975 in contingency, for a total of $241,727.
