New Police Station

The funding, program management, and delivery decisions for Beaumont’s new police station, CIP PS-07.

Phase I program management is authorized

On October 20, 2026, Council unanimously approved Tilden-Coil Constructors as the City’s program manager and owner’s representative. The motion authorized $241,727 for Phase I. Each later phase must return to Council for funding and authorization before work on that phase begins.

Later-phase approvals

The $5,437,851 overall ceiling applies to Tilden-Coil’s program-management agreement. Staff described a delivery approach in which the City would hold separate prime construction contracts. Architect selection, later program-management phases, and construction awards have their own approval steps.

Site and conceptual design

In February 2025, Council approved the purchase agreement for the approximately 30-acre City property at Beaumont Avenue and Cougar Way. The March 2026 staff report used about 10 acres as the working assumption for the station and sought Council direction on uses for the remaining land. The FY 2025 annual report describes a nearly six-acre station concept. The dated records use different footprint assumptions. Final site and design documents would establish the approved campus boundary and building program.
