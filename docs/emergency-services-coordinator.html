Emergency Services Coordinator

Beaumont’s two-year agreement with Riverside County for on-site emergency management and access to County support.

Key records and decisions

August 18, 2026 · Council approval

Council unanimously approved the Riverside County Emergency Management Department agreement at $195,000 per year, for a two-year base cost of $390,000.

Council minutes, item J.4 → · Meeting briefing →

Service scope and funding

The agreement calls for 40 on-site hours per week to support emergency planning, training, grants, and disaster cost recovery, along with access to County duty-officer support. Staff identified first-year funding in the Police Department budget. Overtime and extraordinary costs may be billed separately.
